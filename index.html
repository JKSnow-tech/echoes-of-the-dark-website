This Privacy Policy describes how 57Game Studio and TPKGameDeveloper collect, use, and protect information when you use our game "Echoes of the Dark".

1. Information We Collect

  • Epic Account ID (via Epic Online Services)
  • Gameplay progress data
  • Achievement unlock status

2. How We Use Information

  • To enable Epic ID login
  • To provide achievement tracking
  • To improve gameplay experience

3. Third-Party Services

Our game uses Epic Online Services provided by Epic Games. Epic's privacy policy can be found here:

https://www.epicgames.com/site/en-US/privacypolicy

4. Data Security

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Authentication and online functionality are handled securely through Epic Online Services infrastructure.
